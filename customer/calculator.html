How We Calculate Your Savings

  • FoodBridge prices are estimated at 30% of the original meal price, rounded to the nearest NT dollar.
  • Annual estimates assume 52 weeks.
  • Average monthly estimates are calculated by dividing annual totals by 12.
  • Potential food waste avoided is estimated using the number of surplus meals and their assumed average weight.
  • Results are illustrative estimates, not guaranteed financial savings or measured environmental outcomes.
